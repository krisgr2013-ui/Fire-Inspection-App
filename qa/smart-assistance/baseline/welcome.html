BEFORE YOU START

A few straight answers.

Who is Gold Standard built for?

Fire protection service businesses working with portable extinguishers and kitchen suppression systems, with field crews and office staff managing the same work.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Gold Standard works in a browser on phone and desktop. The demo shows the app with sample business records.

What happens after I create an account?

Start your 30-day free trial with no payment or credit card required to sign up. Follow the account confirmation instructions, sign in, and set up your company. Review the available subscription options inside the app before choosing a paid plan.

Does it replace manufacturer instructions or adopted codes?

No. Your technicians remain responsible for following applicable codes, manufacturer procedures, and requirements of the authority having jurisdiction.
